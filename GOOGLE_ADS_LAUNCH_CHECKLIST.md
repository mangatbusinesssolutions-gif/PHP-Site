# Google Ads launch checklist

Google requires ad destinations to be functional, useful, accessible and easy to navigate. Google also prohibits misleading representation, unclear business identity, dishonest pricing, unavailable offers and insufficient original content.

Before launch:
- Replace example domain everywhere.
- Publish the genuine business name, address, phone and email.
- Ensure the phone is active and appropriate for the targeted country.
- Verify the business and payment information.
- Use original PC World Geek branding; do not imitate Currys/PC World/Geek Squad.
- Do not imply affiliation, endorsement or authorisation without a real agreement.
- Ensure every advertised service is actually available.
- Make 24/7 claims only if the business genuinely provides 24/7 availability.
- Make "instant", "real-time", "same-day" or "guaranteed" claims only when they are true and supportable.
- Display one-off vs recurring billing clearly.
- Display the full subscription price and billing interval.
- Publish final cancellation/refund terms.
- Connect the contact and booking systems.
- Test the site on mobile and desktop.
- Ensure Google Ads can crawl the landing pages.
- Ensure final URL and display URL match the actual domain.
- Configure conversion tracking consistently with the privacy/cookie disclosures.
- Add genuine business information and original content to landing pages.
- Never use fake reviews, fake scarcity, fake system warnings, or fabricated technical errors.

Use only claims that are true for the live service.
